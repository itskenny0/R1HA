New features:
* Native dashboards: render your HA Lovelace `lovelace/config` in Compose. Thirteen card types covered (Entities, Tile, Button, Light, Gauge, Markdown, Heading, Glance, Weather Forecast, Horizontal Stack, Vertical Stack, Grid, Conditional) with a drag-and-drop editor and per-view overrides. Unsupported cards fall back to the Lovelace WebView in one tap.
* Per-card customization: long-press the card-stack hamburger to open a nested CUSTOMIZE sheet (NAME / GLYPH / POSITION / TAP / LIGHTING / LOCK / RESET) with live preview, 9-way position-pip picker, per-card glyph override, and per-card tap-action override.
* Alarm control panel card with optional per-entity PIN keypad; PIN stored encrypted at rest.
* Native registry browsers: Devices, Integrations (with per-entry RELOAD), Blueprints (with import-by-URL), Logs (with level filter), Users, Tags (NFC and QR), Statistics (long-term recorder chart).

Polish:
* IoT Camera Mode sectioned page, soft-confirm on disable, advanced settings collapsed by default.
* IoT Sensors Mode: collapsible categories, MQTT topics surfaced inline.
* MQTT settings: connection-state pill, inline test result, per-feature hierarchy.
* Sync: unified onboarding into 01/02/03 flow, conflict-model card, SYNC NOW chip, push-overwrite escape hatch, probe-status indicator.
* Key Bindings: conflict warnings, customized rail, animated capture overlay.

New in IoT Sensors:
* Device sensors and controls publish to HA via MQTT discovery (battery, screen state, ringer, charging, etc.) without any HA-side YAML.

Fixes:
* IoT Camera Mode self-heals capture after camera loss.
* IoT Camera Mode service teardown no longer ANRs the main thread.
